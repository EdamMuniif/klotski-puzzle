How to play

  1. Swipe or drag blocks on touchscreens and with a mouse. On desktop, you can also select a block and press the arrow keys.
  2. Move blocks into the empty spaces.
  3. Blocks slide one square at a time. They cannot rotate or overlap.
  4. Guide the large red 2×2 block to the glowing bottom exit.
  5. Every starting board is solvable. Begin at Easy Level 1. Each victory unlocks the next level. After Easy 9, continue to Medium 1, then Hard 1.
  6. Each level has only 4 hints total. Restarting does not restore them.
Level-up journey: Easy (10–26 moves) → Medium (87–96 moves) → Hard (60–116 moves). The four hints for each level are saved on this device and do not reset on restart. Previous Hard-mode scores and hints are preserved.
