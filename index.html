GAME GUIDE

How to play

  1. Move the blocks. Drag or swipe a piece on a phone or tablet. On desktop, drag with a mouse or select a piece and use the arrow keys.
  2. Use empty spaces. Blocks move one cell at a time; they cannot overlap or rotate.
  3. Reach the exit. Slide the red 2×2 block to the glowing bottom-center exit.
  4. Level up. Clear Easy 1–9, Medium 1–9, then Hard 1–12. Finished levels can be replayed.
  5. Use hints carefully. Each level has four total hints. Undo, restart, or reloading will not restore spent hints.
