How to play

  1. Swipe or drag blocks on touchscreens and with a mouse. On desktop, you can also select a block and press the arrow keys.
  2. Move blocks into the empty spaces.
  3. Blocks slide one square at a time. They cannot rotate or overlap.
  4. Guide the large blue 2×2 block to the glowing bottom exit.
  5. Every starting board is solvable. Clear a level to unlock the next.
  6. Each level has only 4 hints total. Restarting does not restore them.
Hard mode: No warm-up levels. The shortest solutions range from 60 to 116 moves. Each level has 4 hints in total; the remaining count is saved on this device and does not reset when restarting. Hints calculate a shortest route from your current board.
